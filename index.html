Welcome to JotDrop

  1. Type or paste your notes in the box. One thing per line is easiest.
  2. Tap Split into items and check that each one looks right.
  3. Tap Add. They show up in your Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

Keep it on your home screen: on iPhone tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On Android tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

One-time setup: connect your Google account bridge in settings before adding anything.

No AI? How to jot so it splits right
  • One item per line.
  • Extra detail for the item above: start the line with a space, -, • or >. It becomes that item's notes.
  • Or leave a blank line between items. Every line inside a group then stays one item (first line is the title).
  • All on one line? Commas split it.
  • A time like "3pm" or "at 5" makes it an event. A day like "fri" or "10/20" becomes the due date.
  • Priority: !!! or p1 = high, !! or p2 = medium, p3 = low. Words like "urgent" or "someday" work too.

Priority colors

Events get the color on Google Calendar. Google Tasks can't be colored, so tasks get a matching colored dot at the start of the title.

Pick my own colors

Google Calendar only has 11 event colors. Any color you pick stays exact in this app and uses the closest of the 11 on your calendar.

AI splitting

If AI is off, has no key, or fails, JotDrop splits by new lines, then commas.

Family AI (owner only)

Lets everyone using your link pick "Family AI" without their own key. Usage is billed to this key. The key stays on Google's servers and is never sent to anyone's phone.

Move settings to another device

On iPhone, the home screen app and Safari keep separate settings. This code carries everything over, including your keys, so treat it like a password.
